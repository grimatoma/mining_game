Hearthlands Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 23, 2026

Overview

Hearthlands is a single-player tile-matching and settlement-building game. This policy explains how the Android app handles information when you play.

Information we collect

Hearthlands does not collect personal information. The app has no account registration, sign-in, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or in-app purchase system.

The app saves gameplay progress and preferences, such as game state and settings, locally on your device. This local save data is used only to let the game remember your progress and settings. It is not sent to the developer or to another service.

How information is used and shared

Because Hearthlands does not collect personal information, it does not sell, rent, share, or disclose personal information to third parties.

Permissions and network access

The Android app includes internet permission required by its application wrapper. Hearthlands does not use that permission to collect or transmit personal information. The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

Your choices

You can reset local game progress from the app's settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored app data from your device, subject to your device's backup settings.

Children's privacy

Hearthlands does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other players.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
